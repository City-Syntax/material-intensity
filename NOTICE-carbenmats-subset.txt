carbenmats_subset_GPL3.csv (51 records, ID_marked prefix "G-")

This file is a MODIFIED VERSION of a subset of the CarbEnMats / GBDB
("Global Buildings Database Seed on Whole Life Carbon Emissions, Energy
Performance, and Material Intensity") dataset by Martin Röck, Andreas
Sørensen, Maria Balouktsi, and colleagues:

  Röck, M. et al.: A Global Database on Whole Life Carbon, Energy and
  Material Intensity of Buildings (CarbEnMats-Buildings), 2023.
  Preprint: https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-3373442/v1
  Source repository: https://github.com/mroeck/carbenmats-buildings
  (GNU General Public License v3.0)

Modifications made by Wanyu Pei and Yu Qian Ang (National University of
Singapore), completed 2026: 51 records were selected from the original
CarbEnMats/GBDB release (those passing this study's attribute-completeness
and target-material filters, described in the accompanying paper, Sect. 2.3),
and the following fields were relabelled/recoded into this study's
harmonised schema: building function/typology, structural system code,
country, construction period, and the five target material-intensity
columns (concrete, steel, wood, brick, glass). No material-intensity values
were altered; only field names, categorical codes, and units were
harmonised. The original, unmodified CarbEnMats/GBDB dataset remains
available at the source repository above ("Corresponding Source" per
GPL-3.0 Sect. 1).
